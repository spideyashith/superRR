## 8. Assumptions (carried into every stage)

1. **Runs fields.** `runs` is runs off the bat. Delivery total = `runs + extras1_runs + extras_2_runs`. Validated by reconstructing 322 run-margin results in the analysis-ready set with zero mismatches.
2. **No-balls.** `Overstep` and `other` are no-ball families: the delivery is illegal, the batter faces it and can score off it, and the bowler is charged the bat runs plus the extra. `other` rows behave exactly like `Overstep` (over_ball_num > 6, 1 penalty run, bat runs possible).
3. **Wides.** A wide is not a ball faced and is charged entirely to the bowler. 40 wickets fall on wides (run out/stumping); these are wickets lost but not bowler wickets.
4. **Byes and leg-byes.** Not charged to the bowler; they are team extras. One row (`match_id` 37099) is tagged `legbyes` with zero extra runs and one run off the bat; the additive formula still reconstructs the innings total.
5. **Penalty.** The single `Penalty` row (match 16753, 5 runs) is not a delivery: no legal ball, no batter ball faced, no bowler charge. That match is also DLS-shortened and excluded.
6. **Super overs.** Innings 3 and 4 are super overs. They are excluded entirely, and matches decided by a super over or a one-over eliminator are excluded from modelling (regulation innings were tied).
7. **Scope.** IPL (`comp_name` matching `IPL YYYY`) plus `match_format == 'T20I'`. The two other T20 competitions (IDs 2670 and 3289) are excluded: the PRD scopes the data to IPL + T20I, and one of them contains players missing from `playing_eleven`.
8. **Analysis-ready match set.** In-scope, a winner exists, both regulation innings exist and are complete (all balls used, all out, or target reached), not tied, no super over, and not DLS/shortened (`first_inn_revised_overs`, `second_inn_revised_overs` and `revised_target` all null, plus a result-text DLS check). This leaves 766 matches. The completeness rule catches 11 otherwise-unflagged rain-truncated games. Ball-level rows from excluded matches remain in the cleaned fact table but are flagged and are never used for modelling.
9. **Wickets.** `is_wicket` counts every dismissal including run out, retire out and obstructing the field. Bowler wickets are restricted to Bowled, Caught, LBW, Stump and Hit Wicket. One retire-out row has no `dismissed_player_id`; it is counted as a team wicket but attributed to nobody.
10. **Player names.** The canonical output name is `competition_team_player_mapping.player_name` (the player registry), falling back to the ball-by-ball name. Only one ID differs between the two sources.
11. **Player IDs.** IDs are global across competitions and seasons in this pack; they are used as the join key and never re-derived from names.
12. **Impact players.** `playing_eleven.is_impact` marks IPL impact players; they are included when they bat or bowl, with `batting_order` from the ball-by-ball file where the XI row is absent.
13. **Dates.** `match_date` is a date, not a timestamp; seasons run 2022 to 2026. No external data is used.
